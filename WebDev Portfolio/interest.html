Movies

Top 4 Movies

The Amazing Spider-Man 2

I love how Andrew Garfield's Spider-Man moves, and this movie is emotionally impactful. For me, this is the best Spider-Man movie.

Spider-Man Brand New Day

Another Spider-Man movie, but this time with Tom Holland as Spider-Man. This brings a fresh start for Peter Parker after what happened in the previous film. This movie also brings new and familiar characters into the Marvel universe.

Now You See Me

One of the best magic shows I have watched. Even after rewatching it so many times, it still amazes me with the different magic tricks they pulled.

The Greatest Showman

One of the musical movies I truly enjoyed. This movie brings countless memorable songs, along with Hugh Jackman delivering a one-of-a-kind performance that gives me goosebumps every time I rewatch it.
